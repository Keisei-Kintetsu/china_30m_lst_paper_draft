\begin{abstract}
We present \chinatherm{}, a record of annual 30 m leaf-on daytime land surface temperature (LST) composites across China for 2000--2024. Annual thermal targets derived from quality-screened Terra eight-day daytime LST composites were combined with 30 m annual Landsat optical indices, topography and geographic coordinates in a cross-year random forest. Low-frequency residual adjustment calibrated the regional thermal background. The record comprises 25 annual products, each containing 91 tiles of 4° by 4°. Spatial-block validation of the random forest yielded a correlation of 0.904 and an RMSE of 3.967~°C. The median annual nationwide spatial correlation with Landsat 8 LST during 2013--2024 was 0.864. Ground evaluation across 23 sites and 131 site-years, predominantly in the Heihe basin, yielded a correlation of 0.840 and an RMSE of 5.397~°C; unmanned aerial vehicle thermal imagery further evaluated local thermal patterns. The record supports long-term comparisons and local-scale studies of leaf-on daytime land-surface thermal environments across China.
\end{abstract}
